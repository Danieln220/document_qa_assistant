# Fleet Maintenance Manual

Document RER-MNT-002, Revision 11, issued February 3, 2026 by the Fleet Service Supervisor. This manual sets out the service intervals, inspection routines and workshop procedures for the Ridgeline Equipment Rentals fleet. It applies to all yard technicians and workshop staff at the Boise Main Yard, Nampa and Twin Falls branches. Where this manual and the manufacturer's service manual differ, follow whichever interval is shorter and tell the Fleet Service Supervisor.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 Every item in the rental fleet must be safe, clean and fully working each time it leaves a branch. A breakdown on a customer site costs Ridgeline far more than the service that would have prevented it: a replacement delivery, a rental credit, and often the customer's next order.

1.2 This manual covers: the equipment tag system (Section 2); pre-rental and post-rental checks (Sections 3 and 4); the scheduled service intervals for each equipment type (Sections 5 to 11); fluids and parts (Section 12); workshop safety (Section 13); and service records (Section 14).

1.3 Service intervals in this manual are measured in engine hours from the hour meter, or in calendar time, whichever comes first. Where an item has no hour meter, the calendar interval applies.

## 2. Equipment Tag System

2.1 Every item in the yard carries one colored tag on the key or the control panel. The tag shows at a glance whether the item may be rented.

| Tag | Meaning | Who may change it |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Inspected, serviced and ready to rent. | Yard technician after the pre-rental check |
| Yellow | Returned and waiting for post-rental inspection, cleaning or a minor service. Do not rent. | Any staff member on check-in |
| Red | Out of service. Fault found, damage found, or service overdue. Do not rent under any circumstances. | Only a qualified technician may remove a red tag, after repair |

2.2 A red tag must show the date, the technician's initials and a short description of the fault. A red-tagged item must be parked in the workshop bay or the red-tag row, never in the ready line.

2.3 Counter staff must check that an item has a green tag before creating a checkout report. If an item without a green tag is needed urgently, call the workshop; do not release it.

## 3. Pre-Rental Check (Every Rental)

3.1 The pre-rental check is carried out before an item is given a green tag. It takes about 10 minutes for small equipment and 20 minutes for excavators, skid steers and lifts. Record the result on the checkout report in FleetBook.

3.2 For every item:

- Walk around the item and photograph all four sides. Upload the photographs to the checkout report.
- Check that the operator's manual, safety decals and the Ridgeline ID plate are present and readable.
- Check the engine oil level, coolant level, hydraulic oil level and fuel level. Fill the fuel tank to full.
- Start the engine and let it run for at least 3 minutes. Check for warning lights, unusual noise, smoke and leaks.
- Test every control, including the emergency stop, horn, travel alarm and lights.
- Record the hour meter reading on the checkout report.

3.3 Additional checks for excavators and skid steers:

- Check track tension. On the EX-18 and EX-35, the track sag measured at the middle of the lower span must be between 20 and 25 mm (about 3/4 to 1 inch).
- Check that all bucket teeth and pins are present and secure. Replace worn teeth before they reach the wear line.
- Check the quick coupler locking pin and the safety lock.
- Operate the boom, arm, bucket and blade through their full range and check for drift.
- Check the seat belt and the operator presence switch.

3.4 Additional checks for generators and light towers: run the generator under a test load of at least 50% of rated output for 15 minutes using the load bank, and check the voltage and frequency readings are within the limits on the data plate. Test every outlet and the ground-fault protection (GFCI) test button.

3.5 Additional checks for scissor lifts: confirm the battery is fully charged, test the platform raise and lower controls from both the platform and the ground, test the emergency lowering valve, the tilt alarm and the pothole protection bars.

## 4. Post-Rental Inspection (Every Return)

4.1 Every item returned is given a yellow tag on check-in. The post-rental inspection must be completed within 2 business hours, following the Returns and Damages Policy.

4.2 Compare the item against the checkout photographs. Record any new damage with dated photographs and assign a damage category (A, B or C) on the inspection report in FleetBook.

4.3 Record the hour meter reading and the fuel level. The difference in hours is used to check the included-hours allowance on weekly and four-week rentals.

4.4 Wash the item in the wash bay. Use the oil separator bay only. Do not wash electrical panels, generator alternators or scissor lift control boxes with the pressure washer; wipe them clean.

4.5 Check whether a scheduled service is due within the next 50 engine hours or 30 days. If it is, carry out the service before the item is given a green tag.

4.6 If no fault is found and no service is due, carry out the pre-rental check and change the tag to green.

## 5. Mini and Micro Excavators (EX-18, EX-35)

5.1 The EX-18 and EX-35 are the most rented items in the fleet and the most expensive to repair when neglected. Follow the intervals below strictly.

| Task | EX-18 | EX-35 |
|---|---|---|
| Grease all pivot points (boom, arm, bucket, blade, swing) | Every rental and every 10 hours | Every rental and every 10 hours |
| Check and adjust track tension | Every rental | Every rental |
| Clean air filter element | Every 50 hours | Every 50 hours |
| Change engine oil and oil filter | Every 250 hours | Every 250 hours |
| Replace fuel filter and drain water separator | Every 500 hours | Every 500 hours |
| Replace hydraulic return filter | Every 500 hours | Every 500 hours |
| Replace air filter element | Every 500 hours or 12 months | Every 500 hours or 12 months |
| Change hydraulic oil and clean suction strainer | Every 1,000 hours or 12 months | Every 1,000 hours or 12 months |
| Change coolant | Every 2,000 hours or 24 months | Every 2,000 hours or 24 months |
| Check swing bearing and slew ring bolts | Every 500 hours | Every 500 hours |

5.2 The EX-35 hydraulic oil must be changed every 1,000 engine hours or every 12 months, whichever comes first. The hydraulic tank holds 9.5 US gallons; the complete system holds approximately 14 US gallons. Use ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil only. On the EX-18, the hydraulic tank holds 5.2 US gallons.

5.3 When changing hydraulic oil, lower all attachments to the ground and release pressure from the system by operating each control lever with the engine off and the key in the ON position. Clean the area around the tank cap before opening it. Remove and clean the suction strainer, replace the return filter, and refill to the middle of the sight glass. Run the engine at low idle and operate each function slowly five times to bleed air, then recheck the level.

5.4 Engine oil for both models is SAE 15W-40 diesel engine oil (API CK-4). The EX-35 engine holds 7.4 US quarts including the filter; the EX-18 holds 3.9 US quarts.

5.5 Rubber tracks must be replaced when the lugs are worn to 5 mm or less, when steel cords are visible, or when there is a cut longer than 3 inches in the outer edge. Replace tracks in pairs.

5.6 After any rental where the excavator worked in mud, clay or water, clean the undercarriage and check for stones lodged between the sprocket and the track.

## 6. Skid Steer Loaders (SL-750)

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Grease all pivot points and quick-attach | Every rental and every 10 hours |
| Check tire pressure (45 psi) | Every rental |
| Check and clean radiator and oil cooler fins | Every 50 hours |
| Change engine oil and filter | Every 250 hours |
| Replace fuel filter | Every 500 hours |
| Replace hydraulic filter | Every 500 hours |
| Change hydraulic and hydrostatic oil | Every 800 hours or 12 months |
| Check drive chain tension in chaincase | Every 500 hours |
| Change coolant | Every 2,000 hours or 24 months |

6.1 The SL-750 uses a combined hydraulic and hydrostatic system. Use only the approved universal tractor hydraulic fluid (UTHF) listed in Section 12. Mixing fluids can damage the drive motors.

6.2 Check the seat bar, the lift arm support device and the rear-view camera on every pre-rental check. A skid steer without a working seat bar interlock must be red-tagged.

## 7. Generators (GN-7500)

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Load test at 50% or more for 15 minutes | Every rental |
| Check oil level | Every rental and every 8 hours of running |
| Change engine oil | Every 100 hours |
| Clean air filter | Every 50 hours |
| Replace spark plug | Every 300 hours |
| Replace air filter element | Every 300 hours |
| Clean spark arrestor | Every 100 hours |
| Replace fuel filter | Every 300 hours or 12 months |

7.1 The GN-7500 runs on unleaded gasoline only (87 octane or higher). The fuel tank holds 6.6 US gallons, giving about 10 hours of running at 50% load.

7.2 Engine oil is SAE 10W-30. The engine holds 1.2 US quarts. The engine has a low-oil shutdown; if a customer reports that the generator stops by itself, check the oil level first.

7.3 Before the load test, check that the grounding terminal and ground rod clamp are present. Record the voltage (target 240 V / 120 V plus or minus 5%) and frequency (target 60 Hz plus or minus 1 Hz) on the checkout report.

## 8. Plate Compactors (PC-20)

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Check engine oil | Every rental |
| Change engine oil | Every 50 hours |
| Check exciter oil level | Every 100 hours |
| Change exciter oil | Every 300 hours |
| Check V-belt tension and wear | Every 50 hours |
| Clean air filter | Every rental in dusty conditions, otherwise every 25 hours |

8.1 Check the rubber mat (if fitted) for tears. A torn mat is charged under the Returns and Damages Policy.

8.2 Check that the shock mounts between the base plate and the upper frame are not cracked. A compactor with a cracked shock mount must be red-tagged, because the handle vibration will exceed safe limits for the operator.

## 9. Electric Scissor Lifts (SC-26)

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Charge batteries to full | After every rental |
| Check battery electrolyte level and top up with distilled water | Weekly |
| Test tilt alarm, pothole protection and emergency lowering | Every rental |
| Inspect scissor pins, guard rails and platform gate | Every rental |
| Lubricate scissor pins and slide pads | Every 250 hours |
| Change hydraulic oil | Every 1,000 hours or 24 months |
| Annual inspection by a qualified inspector (ANSI A92.22 / A92.24) | Every 12 months |

9.1 Never leave a scissor lift discharged. Batteries left below 20% charge for more than a few days lose capacity permanently. Put the lift on charge as soon as it is checked in, even before the post-rental inspection.

9.2 An SC-26 whose annual inspection is overdue must be red-tagged. The inspection date is shown on the decal next to the platform control box.

## 10. Trenchers, Saws and Pressure Washers (TR-24, CS-14, PW-4000)

10.1 TR-24 walk-behind trencher: grease the boom pivot and chain every rental; check the digging chain tension every rental; replace worn teeth on the digging chain in sets; change the engine oil every 100 hours.

10.2 CS-14 concrete saw: check the blade guard and the water feed every rental; measure blade wear with the blade gauge on check-in and record it for the wear charge; clean the air filter every rental, because concrete dust quickly blocks it; replace the drive belt every 100 hours.

10.3 PW-4000 pressure washer: flush the pump with clean water after every rental; check the pump oil every rental and change it every 200 hours; check the high-pressure hose and gun for damage; in winter, run pump antifreeze through the system before storage.

## 11. Light Towers (LT-60)

11.1 Check the mast raise and lower mechanism, the mast lock pin and the cable condition every rental. Test all four lamps.

11.2 The LT-60 generator engine follows the same service intervals as the GN-7500 (Section 7), except that the engine runs on diesel. Use ultra-low-sulfur diesel only.

11.3 Check the trailer lights, safety chains, tire pressure and wheel nut torque before every delivery.

## 12. Fluids and Parts

12.1 Use only the fluids listed below. If a fluid is not in stock, tell the Fleet Service Supervisor; do not substitute.

| Fluid | Used in |
|---|---|
| SAE 15W-40 diesel engine oil, API CK-4 | EX-18, EX-35, SL-750, LT-60 engines |
| SAE 10W-30 engine oil | GN-7500, PC-20, TR-24, CS-14, PW-4000 engines |
| ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil | EX-18, EX-35, SC-26 |
| Universal tractor hydraulic fluid (UTHF) | SL-750 |
| Pump oil, SAE 30 non-detergent | PW-4000 pump |
| Extended-life coolant, 50/50 premix | All liquid-cooled engines |
| Lithium EP2 grease | All grease points |

12.2 Parts are ordered through FleetBook using the parts request form. Parts under $250 may be ordered by any technician. Parts from $250 to $2,000 need the Branch Manager's approval. Parts over $2,000 need the Fleet Service Supervisor's approval.

12.3 Used oil, filters and coolant must be stored in the labeled containers in the fluids store and collected by the approved recycling contractor. Never pour fluids into drains or onto the ground. Oil spills are cleaned with the absorbent from the spill kit and the absorbent is disposed of as oily waste.

## 13. Workshop Safety

13.1 Personal protective equipment is required in the workshop at all times: safety glasses, steel-toe boots and high-visibility vest. Add hearing protection when engines are running indoors, and nitrile gloves when handling oil and fuel.

13.2 Lockout and tagout. Before any work on hydraulic, electrical or moving parts: switch off the engine; remove the key and keep it in your pocket; lower all attachments to the ground; release hydraulic pressure; disconnect the battery for electrical work; and attach your personal lock and "Do Not Operate" tag to the key switch or battery isolator. Only the person who attached a lock may remove it.

13.3 Never work under a raised boom, bucket, lift arm or scissor platform unless it is supported by the correct mechanical support (boom lock, lift arm support device or maintenance prop). Hydraulic pressure alone is not support.

13.4 Engines must not be run indoors unless the exhaust extraction hose is connected. Fuel equipment only in the outdoor fuel area.

13.5 Report every injury, near miss and fluid spill to the Branch Manager on the day it happens, using the incident form in FleetBook.

## 14. Service Records

14.1 Every service, repair, inspection and tag change is recorded in FleetBook against the item's Ridgeline ID. Records must include the date, the hour meter reading, the work done, the parts and fluids used, and the technician's name.

14.2 FleetBook shows a service warning when an item is within 50 hours or 30 days of a scheduled service. Items with an overdue service are automatically blocked from new rentals until the service is recorded.

14.3 Service records are kept for the life of the item plus 3 years after it leaves the fleet.

14.4 Questions about this manual should be directed to the Fleet Service Supervisor at the Boise Main Yard.
