# Returns and Damages Policy

Document RER-OPS-014, Revision 4, approved by the Operations Manager on January 12, 2026. This policy explains how Ridgeline Equipment Rentals checks in returned Equipment, how damage is assessed and charged, and what the Customer can do if they disagree with a charge. It forms part of every rental contract together with the Standard Terms and Conditions (document RER-LEG-001). Where this policy and the Standard Terms and Conditions differ, the Standard Terms and Conditions apply.

## 1. Returning Equipment

1.1 Equipment must be returned to the branch that issued the rental contract unless a different branch has been agreed in writing and shown on the contract. A one-way return fee of $60 applies where Equipment is returned to a different branch by agreement.

1.2 Returns are accepted during branch opening hours only. Opening hours for each branch are shown in the Branch FAQ. Returns are not accepted at any branch on Sundays or on public holidays.

1.3 After-hours drop-off is available at the Boise Main Yard only, using the secure drop zone next to Gate 2. Keys must be placed in the key drop box marked "Returns". Equipment left in the drop zone is treated as returned at the next branch opening time, not at the time it was left. The Customer remains responsible for the Equipment until it is checked in.

1.4 Equipment must not be left at any branch outside the drop zone, including in the customer parking area or at the front gate. Equipment left outside the drop zone remains the Customer's responsibility until it is found and checked in by Ridgeline staff.

1.5 On return, the Customer should remove all personal items, waste, and spoil from the Equipment and from any buckets and attachments.

## 2. Check-In and Inspection

2.1 Every item of Equipment is inspected by a Ridgeline yard technician within 2 business hours of being checked in. The Customer is welcome to be present at the inspection and is encouraged to do so for excavators, skid steers and lifts.

2.2 The inspection covers: the condition of the body, cab and glass; tracks, tires and undercarriage; buckets, teeth and attachments; hydraulic hoses and fittings; lights, horn, alarms and safety devices; fluid levels and leaks; the fuel level; and the hour meter reading.

2.3 The technician records the result on an inspection report with dated photographs. The inspection report is compared with the checkout report completed when the Equipment left the branch.

2.4 The Customer receives a copy of the inspection report, with photographs, by email within 24 hours of check-in. Where damage is found, the report shows the damage category and the estimated charge.

## 3. Late Returns and Unreturned Equipment

3.1 Late charges are calculated as set out in Section 6 of the Standard Terms and Conditions, at 1.5 times the Daily Rate for each Rental Day or part of a Rental Day that the Equipment is late, after the 59-minute grace period.

3.2 In addition to the late charges, a late return administration fee of $35 is charged once per rental contract whenever any item on the contract is returned late.

3.3 If Equipment is more than 24 hours late and the Customer has not contacted the branch, Ridgeline will attempt to contact the Customer by phone and email, and may use the GPS tracker to locate the Equipment.

3.4 If Equipment has not been returned within 72 hours after the end of the Rental Period and the Customer cannot be reached, Ridgeline will treat the Equipment as unreturned. Ridgeline may then recover the Equipment under Section 14 of the Standard Terms and Conditions and may report it to the police as stolen. Late charges continue until the Equipment is recovered.

## 4. Cleaning

4.1 Equipment should be returned in the same clean condition in which it was supplied, allowing for normal site dirt. Mud and spoil should be knocked off tracks, buckets and wheels before return.

4.2 Where Equipment is returned with excessive mud, concrete, paint, or other material that requires more than 15 minutes of cleaning, a cleaning fee applies: $75 per item for standard Equipment, and $150 per item for excavators and skid steers.

4.3 Where concrete, mortar or paint has set on the Equipment and cannot be removed by washing, the cost of removal or replacement of the affected parts is charged as damage under Section 5.

## 5. Damage Categories

5.1 Every item of damage found at inspection is placed in one of three categories:

| Category | Description | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| A: Normal wear | Light scuffs, scratches and paint marks from normal use; normal wear of teeth, pads and tracks. | No charge |
| B: Moderate damage | Damage that can be repaired in the Ridgeline workshop, such as bent panels, cut hoses, broken lights or broken teeth. | Parts at cost plus labor at $95 per hour, plus 15% handling |
| C: Major damage | Damage that needs outside repair, major parts, or replacement of the Equipment. | Repair cost, or replacement at current market value less 10% per year of age |

5.2 Where the Customer accepted the Damage Waiver, the charge for accidental damage in Categories B and C is limited to the $500 deductible per incident, except for the exclusions listed in Section 9.3 of the Standard Terms and Conditions.

## 6. Standard Damage Charges

6.1 For the most common items of damage, Ridgeline uses the fixed charges below so that Customers receive a quick and predictable invoice. These charges include parts, labor and handling. They apply whether or not the Damage Waiver was accepted, except that the total charge per incident is capped at the $500 deductible where the waiver was accepted and the damage is covered.

| Item | Charge |
|---|---|
| Excavator bucket tooth, broken or missing | $45 per tooth |
| Hydraulic hose, cut or burst (up to 6 ft) | $160 per hose |
| Cab door glass or side window, cracked or broken | $380 per pane |
| Rubber track, cut or de-laminated (EX-18 / EX-35) | $1,850 per track |
| Work light, broken lens or housing | $65 per light |
| Generator control panel, damaged (GN-7500) | $290 |
| Pressure washer hose or gun, damaged (PW-4000) | $120 |
| Plate compactor rubber mat, torn (PC-20) | $55 |
| Scissor lift platform extension, bent (SC-26) | $420 |
| Lost ignition key | $65 per key |
| Lost remote control or charger | Replacement cost plus $25 handling |

6.2 Damage not listed in the table is charged under Category B or C in Section 5.

## 7. Lost and Stolen Equipment

7.1 If Equipment is lost or stolen during the Rental Period, the Customer must report it to Ridgeline immediately, and to the police within 24 hours. The Customer must give Ridgeline a copy of the police report within 48 hours.

7.2 Rental charges continue until the loss is reported to Ridgeline in writing.

7.3 The Customer is charged the replacement value of the lost Equipment, less 10% per year of age, up to a maximum reduction of 50%. Where the Damage Waiver was accepted and a police report was provided within 48 hours, the charge is limited to the $500 deductible.

## 8. Disputes

8.1 If the Customer disagrees with any charge on the inspection report or final invoice, the Customer must raise the dispute in writing, by email to disputes@ridgelinerentals.example or by letter to the Main Yard, within 5 business days of receiving the inspection report.

8.2 The dispute should include the rental contract number, the item being disputed, and any photographs or other evidence the Customer relies on.

8.3 The Branch Manager will review the dispute and reply within 5 business days. Where the dispute is not resolved, the Customer may ask for a review by the Operations Manager, whose decision is final for Ridgeline's internal process.

8.4 Disputes raised after 5 business days are reviewed only at Ridgeline's discretion.

## 9. Deposit Refunds

9.1 The security deposit authorization is released, or any balance refunded, within 5 business days after the final invoice is issued.

9.2 Refunds are made to the original payment card only. Ridgeline does not give cash refunds.

9.3 Where a dispute is open, Ridgeline may hold the part of the deposit equal to the disputed amount until the dispute is resolved.

Approved: Operations Manager, Ridgeline Equipment Rentals, LLC.
