# Equipment Rental Agreement — Standard Terms and Conditions

Document RER-LEG-001, Revision 7, effective March 1, 2026. These Standard Terms and Conditions apply to every rental contract issued by Ridgeline Equipment Rentals, LLC ("Ridgeline", "we", "us") at any of its branches, whether the contract is signed at the counter, on a delivery tablet, or accepted electronically through an online account. The person or business named on the rental contract is referred to as the "Customer". Each rental contract, together with these terms, the current Rate Schedule, and the Returns and Damages Policy, forms the entire agreement between Ridgeline and the Customer.

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement the following words have the meanings shown below. Headings are for convenience only and do not change the meaning of any section.

- "Equipment" means each item listed on the rental contract, including all attachments, buckets, hoses, cords, chargers, keys, manuals, and accessories supplied with it.
- "Rental Period" means the period starting at the time the Equipment leaves the branch or is delivered to the Customer's site, and ending when the Equipment is returned to a Ridgeline branch and checked in by Ridgeline staff, or collected by Ridgeline from the Customer's site.
- "Rental Day" means a period of 24 consecutive hours starting at the checkout time printed on the rental contract.
- "Daily Rate", "Weekly Rate" and "Four-Week Rate" mean the rates shown in the Rate Schedule in Section 4 or on the rental contract.
- "Business Day" means Monday to Friday, excluding the public holidays on which Ridgeline branches are closed.
- "Authorized Operator" means a person aged 18 or over who has been named by the Customer and who is competent and, where required by law, certified to operate the Equipment.

## 2. Eligibility and Identification

2.1 To open a rental contract, an individual Customer must be at least 21 years of age and must present a valid government-issued photo identification and a valid driver's license. The name on the identification must match the name on the payment card presented.

2.2 A business Customer must provide its legal name, business address, a contact person, and either an approved Ridgeline commercial account number or a valid payment card. Ridgeline may request proof of general liability insurance before releasing any Equipment.

2.3 Ridgeline may refuse to rent any Equipment to any person at its sole discretion, including where the Customer has an overdue balance, has previously returned Equipment damaged without payment, or appears unable to transport or operate the Equipment safely.

2.4 The Customer confirms that all information supplied on the rental contract is true and complete. Ridgeline may run a card authorization and an identity check before releasing Equipment.

## 3. Rental Period and Rental Days

3.1 Rental charges are calculated in Rental Days of 24 hours from the checkout time. A rental that starts at 7:40 AM on Tuesday ends one Rental Day later at 7:40 AM on Wednesday.

3.2 Half-day rates are available only for the items marked "4-hour rate available" in the Rate Schedule. A half-day rental is 4 consecutive hours and must be returned within the same branch opening hours.

3.3 Weekend rental: Equipment picked up after 12:00 noon on Saturday and returned before 8:00 AM on the following Monday is charged at 1.5 Rental Days, regardless of the number of hours used. The weekend rate does not apply on holiday weekends.

3.4 Weekly and Four-Week Rates apply automatically where they result in a lower charge than the equivalent number of Daily Rates. A week is 7 consecutive Rental Days. A four-week period is 28 consecutive Rental Days.

3.5 The Customer may ask to extend a rental by calling the issuing branch before the end of the current Rental Period. An extension is only confirmed when the branch issues an updated contract number. Extensions requested after the end of the Rental Period are treated as late returns under Section 6.

3.6 Equipment use is not limited by engine hours on Daily Rates. On Weekly and Four-Week Rates, each Rental Day includes up to 8 engine hours. Engine hours above that allowance, as shown on the hour meter, are charged at one-eighth (1/8) of the Daily Rate per additional hour.

## 5. Deposits, Payment and Invoicing

5.1 A security deposit is taken at checkout as a card authorization, or added to the invoice for commercial account holders. The deposit amounts are shown in the Rate Schedule. Where a rental contract includes several items, the deposits are added together, up to a maximum of $2,500 per contract.

5.2 The deposit is not a limit on the Customer's liability. Ridgeline may apply the deposit to any amount due under this Agreement, including rental charges, late charges, fuel, cleaning, and damage.

5.3 Individual Customers pay the estimated rental charges at checkout. The final invoice is issued after the Equipment is checked in and inspected. Any difference is charged to, or refunded to, the payment card on file.

5.4 Approved commercial account holders are invoiced on the first Business Day after the Equipment is checked in, and invoices are due within 30 days of the invoice date. Overdue invoices carry a late payment charge of 1.5% per month on the overdue balance, and Ridgeline may suspend the account until the balance is paid.

5.5 Accepted payment methods are listed in the Branch FAQ. Ridgeline does not accept personal checks for rentals.

5.6 An environmental fee of 2.5% of the rental charges, up to a maximum of $40 per contract, is added to every invoice to cover the disposal of used oil, filters, and cleaning water.

## 6. Late Returns

6.1 The Customer must return the Equipment to the issuing branch, or make it available for collection, by the end of the Rental Period shown on the rental contract.

6.2 Grace period. Equipment returned no more than 59 minutes after the end of the Rental Period is treated as returned on time.

6.3 Late charge. Equipment returned after the grace period is charged a late charge of 1.5 times the Daily Rate for each Rental Day, or part of a Rental Day, that the Equipment is late. For example, a mini excavator (EX-35, Daily Rate $385) returned two Rental Days late is charged a late charge of $1,155 (2 x 1.5 x $385). Late charges are in addition to the rental charges for the original Rental Period.

6.4 Late charges apply even where the Weekly or Four-Week Rate was used for the original Rental Period. The late charge is always calculated on the Daily Rate.

6.5 Additional fees for late returns, including the late return administration fee and the procedure for unreturned equipment, are set out in the Returns and Damages Policy.

6.6 Where the Customer cannot return the Equipment on time because of a breakdown reported under Section 11, no late charge applies to the period during which Ridgeline was arranging a repair or replacement.

## 7. Delivery and Collection

7.1 Ridgeline delivers and collects Equipment within 25 road miles of the issuing branch for a flat fee of $95 each way. Beyond 25 miles, an additional $3.50 per road mile applies each way. Delivery and collection of Equipment over 7,000 lb operating weight requires a trailer and is quoted separately.

7.2 The Customer must make sure that the delivery site is accessible to a truck and trailer, is level, and is free of overhead power lines within 20 feet of the unloading area. If the driver judges the site unsafe, the Equipment will not be unloaded and the delivery fee remains payable.

7.3 The rental period for delivered Equipment starts when the Equipment is unloaded at the site. For collection, the Customer must call the branch to book a collection; the rental period ends at the time the collection is booked, provided the Equipment is ready and accessible when the driver arrives.

7.4 Where the Customer transports the Equipment, the Customer is responsible for using a suitable vehicle and trailer, for securing the load in line with state regulations, and for any damage that occurs during transport.

## 8. Fuel and Consumables

8.1 All fuel-powered Equipment leaves the branch with a full tank. The Customer must return it with a full tank of the correct fuel type.

8.2 If Equipment is returned with less than a full tank, Ridgeline will refuel it and charge the fuel used at $6.25 per gallon for gasoline or $7.50 per gallon for diesel, plus a refuelling service fee of $25 per item. The amount of fuel used is taken from the fuel gauge or measured at the pump when the tank is filled.

8.3 Damage caused by the wrong fuel type (for example, gasoline in a diesel engine) is not covered by the Damage Waiver and is charged at the full cost of draining, cleaning and repair.

8.4 Engine oil, hydraulic oil and grease are topped up by Ridgeline as part of normal service. On rentals longer than one week, the Customer must check the engine oil daily and call the branch if a top-up is needed. Ridgeline will supply the correct oil at no charge.

8.5 Consumable items such as saw blades, compactor pads, and pressure washer detergent are charged by use or by wear as shown on the Rate Schedule. Diamond saw blades are charged per 1/16 inch of wear.

## 9. Damage Waiver

9.1 The Customer may accept or decline the optional Damage Waiver on the rental contract. If accepted, the Damage Waiver fee is 12% of the rental charges for the Rental Period, including any extensions.

9.2 With the Damage Waiver, the Customer's liability for accidental damage to the Equipment during the Rental Period is limited to a deductible of $500 per incident. Ridgeline covers the remaining cost of repair.

9.3 The Damage Waiver does not cover: (a) theft or loss of Equipment unless a police report is provided within 48 hours; (b) damage caused by misuse, overloading, negligence, or use by anyone who is not an Authorized Operator; (c) damage to tires, tracks or glass caused by punctures, cuts or impact; (d) damage caused by the wrong fuel type; (e) damage during transport by the Customer; (f) water damage from submerging the Equipment; (g) loss of keys, remote controls and small accessories.

9.4 The Damage Waiver is not insurance. It does not cover the Customer's liability to third parties for injury or property damage.

9.5 If the Customer declines the Damage Waiver, the Customer is responsible for the full cost of repair or replacement of damaged or lost Equipment, as assessed under the Returns and Damages Policy.

## 10. Use of Equipment

10.1 The Customer must make sure that the Equipment is operated only by Authorized Operators, in line with the operator's manual supplied with the Equipment, all safety decals, and all applicable laws.

10.2 Ridgeline offers a free 15-minute orientation on every item of Equipment at checkout or delivery. The Customer confirms, by signing the rental contract, that it has received or declined this orientation.

10.3 The Customer must not: (a) sublet, lend, or sell the Equipment; (b) take the Equipment outside the states of Idaho and Oregon without Ridgeline's written consent; (c) use the Equipment to demolish structures unless it is fitted with the correct protective guarding; (d) use the Equipment in water deeper than the top of its tracks or wheels; (e) remove or tamper with hour meters, GPS trackers or safety devices; (f) use the Equipment for any illegal purpose.

10.4 The Customer is responsible for locating underground utilities before any digging by calling 811 at least two Business Days before work begins. Damage to utilities caused by the Customer is not covered by the Damage Waiver.

10.5 Ridgeline Equipment is fitted with GPS tracking. Ridgeline may use location data to locate Equipment for collection, to confirm hours of use, and to recover Equipment that is not returned.

## 11. Breakdowns and Repairs

11.1 If the Equipment breaks down or does not operate correctly, the Customer must stop using it immediately and call the 24/7 breakdown line on (208) 555-0199.

11.2 Ridgeline will arrange a repair or replacement as soon as reasonably possible. If the Equipment cannot be used for more than 4 consecutive hours because of a fault that was not caused by the Customer, Ridgeline will credit the rental charges for the period of downtime.

11.3 The Customer must not attempt repairs, other than checks listed as operator checks in the operator's manual, without Ridgeline's written approval. Ridgeline will not reimburse repair costs that it has not approved in advance.

11.4 Tire punctures and track damage caused on site are the Customer's responsibility.

## 12. Liability and Insurance

12.1 The Customer is responsible for the Equipment from the start to the end of the Rental Period, including loss, theft and damage, subject to the Damage Waiver where accepted.

12.2 The Customer must hold general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence covering its use of the Equipment. On request, the Customer must provide a certificate of insurance naming Ridgeline Equipment Rentals, LLC as additional insured.

12.3 The Customer indemnifies Ridgeline against all claims, losses and costs arising from the use of the Equipment during the Rental Period, except to the extent caused by Ridgeline's own negligence.

12.4 Ridgeline is not liable for any indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit, loss of use, or project delay, arising from a breakdown or late delivery of Equipment. Ridgeline's total liability under any rental contract is limited to the rental charges paid under that contract.

## 13. Cancellation

13.1 A reservation may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled pickup or delivery time.

13.2 Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup or delivery time are charged 50% of one Daily Rate for each item reserved.

13.3 If the Equipment has already left the branch for delivery when the reservation is cancelled, the delivery fee and the collection fee are both charged, in addition to the charge in Section 13.2.

13.4 Ridgeline may cancel a reservation if the reserved Equipment becomes unavailable because of breakdown or late return by another customer. In that case, Ridgeline will offer an equivalent item or a full refund of any amount paid.

## 14. Termination and Repossession

14.1 Ridgeline may end the Rental Period early and recover the Equipment without notice if the Customer breaches this Agreement, if the Equipment is being used unsafely, or if the Customer becomes insolvent.

14.2 The Customer authorizes Ridgeline staff to enter the site where the Equipment is located to recover it during normal working hours. All recovery costs are charged to the Customer.

## 15. Personal Information

15.1 Ridgeline collects the personal information on the rental contract to manage the rental, to collect payment, and to meet its legal obligations. Copies of identification are stored securely and deleted 24 months after the last rental.

15.2 Ridgeline does not sell personal information. Customers may request a copy of their information by writing to the Main Yard office.
