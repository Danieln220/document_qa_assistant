2. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------

2.1 Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking at least two business days ahead for
excavators, skid steers and lifts, especially between April and October. Reservations
can be made by phone, by email, or at the counter.

2.2 What do I need to bring?

Individuals: a valid photo ID, a valid driver's license, and a credit or debit card in
the same name. You must be 21 or older. Businesses: your commercial account number, or a
company card and the name of the person responsible for the rental.

2.3 Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit and debit cards. Cash is accepted
for rental charges up to $500, but the security deposit must be taken on a card. ACH
bank transfer is available for approved commercial accounts. We do not accept personal
checks.

2.4 How do I open a commercial account?

Ask at the counter for a commercial account application, or email the Boise Main Yard.
Approval normally takes 3 to 5 business days and requires two trade references and a
certificate of insurance. Approved accounts are invoiced monthly with 30-day terms.

2.5 Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery and collection within 25 road miles of the branch costs $95 each way;
further than that, $3.50 per additional mile each way. Delivery slots are booked in
2-hour windows starting at 7:00 AM. Same-day delivery is available when booked before
10:00 AM, subject to driver availability.

2.6 Can I tow the equipment myself?

Yes, if your vehicle and hitch are rated for the combined weight. Counter staff will
check your vehicle before loading. Mini excavators and skid steers need a tandem-axle
equipment trailer, which we rent at $65 per day.

2.7 How long is a rental day?

A rental day is 24 hours from the time you pick up. Our weekend rate charges 1.5 days
for equipment picked up after noon on Saturday and returned before 8:00 AM on Monday
(not on holiday weekends).

2.8 Can I extend my rental?

Yes. Call the branch before your rental ends. Your extension is confirmed when we give
you an updated contract number. If you just keep the equipment without calling, late
charges apply.

2.9 What happens if I return it late?

After a 59-minute grace period, late returns are charged extra for each day or part-day
late, and an administration fee also applies. The exact charges are in the rental
agreement and the Returns and Damages Policy. Please call us if you are running late; we
can often extend instead.

2.10 Do I have to refuel it?

Yes. Everything leaves full and should come back full. If not, we refuel it and charge
for the fuel plus a $25 service fee. Generators, compactors, saws and pressure washers
take regular unleaded gasoline. Excavators, skid steers and light towers take diesel.

2.11 Should I buy the Damage Waiver?

It is optional. For 12% of the rental charges, it limits what you pay for accidental
damage to $500 per incident. It does not cover theft without a police report, misuse,
tire or track punctures, or lost keys. Full details are in the rental agreement.

2.12 Will someone show me how to use it?

Yes. Every rental includes a free 15-minute orientation at pickup or on delivery. We can
also send you the operator's manual by email in advance.

2.13 Do I need to call 811 before digging?

Yes. Idaho law requires you to call 811 at least two business days before digging so
underground utilities can be marked. Damage to utilities is your responsibility and is
not covered by the Damage Waiver.

2.14 What if the equipment breaks down?

Stop using it and call the 24/7 breakdown line on (208) 555-0199. If you lose more than
4 hours because of a fault that wasn't your doing, we credit the downtime.

2.15 How do I get my deposit back?

The deposit hold is released, or the balance refunded to the same card, within 5
business days after your final invoice. We cannot refund deposits in cash.

2.16 Are your branches open on holidays?

All branches are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, and close at noon on
Christmas Eve. Equipment due back on a closed day is due at opening time on the next
business day, at no extra charge.
